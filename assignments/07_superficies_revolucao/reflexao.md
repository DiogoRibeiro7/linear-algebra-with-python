# Reflection — Assignment 7

A surface of revolution is completely determined by two ingredients: the generating curve and the axis of revolution. The curve specifies the distance from the axis as a function of the axis coordinate, and the revolution sweeps that curve around the axis to form the surface.

If two generating curves differ, the resulting surfaces differ in radius or shape even if the axis is the same. Likewise, rotating the same curve around different axes produces different surfaces (for example, a curve rotated around the x-axis versus the z-axis).

This perspective links the algebraic equation of the curve to the final 3D geometry: the revolution translates a 2D profile into a 3D surface with rotational symmetry around the chosen axis.
