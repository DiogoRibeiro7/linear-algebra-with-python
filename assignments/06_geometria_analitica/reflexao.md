# Reflection — Assignment 6

Direction vectors determine how a line moves through space, while normal vectors determine the orientation of planes. Two lines are parallel if their direction vectors are proportional and intersecting if their directions are not parallel and the lines meet at a point. In 3D, non-parallel lines can still be skew if they are not coplanar.

For planes, the normal vector defines the plane's orientation. If two planes have parallel normals, they are either parallel or coincident depending on the constant term. If their normals are not parallel, the planes intersect along a line.

These vectors provide a compact, algebraic way to classify relative positions, and they directly connect geometric intuition to linear algebra operations like dot and cross products.
